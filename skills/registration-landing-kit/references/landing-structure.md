# Landing page structure for a registration or booking system

Synthesised from two readable reference sites: STUDIO UNLMTD (studio-unlmtd.com, an Astro site from Nyon, Switzerland) and AW Media (awmedia.marketing, a Next.js site from Sheffield, UK). Patterns are described in the kit's own words; write your own copy.

## Page order (registration landing page)

1. **Skip link**, then **top bar**: logo, one primary action, MENU.
2. **Info bar** (optional, under the top bar): date, place, seats left, "Open in Maps".
3. **Hero**: one promise, one primary action, one reassurance line.
4. **Why / principles**: three short points, numbered.
5. **How it works**: five or six numbered steps.
6. **Proof**: one large number, a short testimonial or logos, only if real.
7. **Register / book**: the form, plus a booking slot picker if time slots matter.
8. **Address and contact**: address block, contact method cards, map link.
9. **Footer**: link groups, language switch, legal and consent settings.

## Patterns from STUDIO UNLMTD (verified from page text)

- **Header:** logo, a short call-to-action button ("Let's talk"), and a MENU button. A skip-to-content link comes first.
- **Full-screen menu:** opens over the page with a CLOSE control and numbered entries (01 to 04), each with a one-line tagline under the title ("See what we mean.", "Give us a limit."). A separate extra entry sits below the numbered list.
- **Section labels:** numbered eyebrow labels such as "01 / THE WHY", "02 / THE WHAT", "03 / THE WHO". They make a long page feel structured.
- **Principles:** three numbered statements, each a short bold heading plus two sentences.
- **Process:** six steps (Think, Explore, Create, Refine, Deliver, Measure), each with a one-line description and a small tag saying who does the work.
- **Big number:** one oversized statistic ("50" years of experience) with a caption.
- **People:** two portrait cards with a question as the heading, a short bio, and lists of background and selected work.
- **Closing action:** a section titled "Your turn." with a single button ("Give us a limit.").
- **Footer:** studio address, an "Open in Maps" link with coordinates, follow links, "Let's talk" links (Book a call, Write to us), language switch (EN and FR), a tagline, copyright, legal link and a "Consent settings" control.
- **Site-level:** white theme colour, Open Graph image 1200x630, language alternates.

## Patterns from AW Media (verified from the contact page text)

- **Contact hero:** a plain-language heading ("Let's build something that actually works."), then one sentence that offers a free 20-minute call or a message and promises no pressure and no jargon.
- **Contact method cards:** Email, Call, WhatsApp and Location, each a direct link (`mailto:`, `tel:`, a WhatsApp link with a prefilled message, a maps search link). Each card has a small label and the value.
- **Follow links:** Instagram, Facebook, Threads, LinkedIn.
- **Form fields:** Name, Email, a three-way choice (AI, Bespoke, Not sure, with "skip if you're not sure" in the label), a select for what they need, a free-text project box, an optional opt-in to a newsletter ("no spam, unsubscribe anytime") and a Send Message button.
- **Second route:** "Or just book a call." with the facts underneath: 20 minutes, free, no obligation.
- **Quick enquiry cards:** three cards that start short guided enquiries (website, logo, social media) for people who want to skip the form.
- **Footer:** company block, link groups (Company, Compare, Services, Industries, Contact), address, a long list of location links, copyright, privacy and cookie policy, and "Cookie settings".
- **Trust cues:** years in business, number of sites built, a sponsorship credit.

## Mapping to a registration system

| Reference pattern | Registration equivalent |
|---|---|
| "Let's talk" button | "Register" or "Reserve a seat" (one verb, same label everywhere) |
| Free 20-minute call | Free to join, takes 2 minutes, cancel any time |
| Three-way choice | Attendee type (Student, Professional, Group) as a segmented control |
| Select "What do you need?" | Session or ticket type |
| Quick enquiry cards | "Register a group" and "Ask a question" shortcuts |
| Six-step process | Choose, Register, Confirm, Remind, Check in, Follow up |
| Big number | Seats left, people registered, or years running (only if true) |
| Address block with maps link | Venue address, entrance notes, map link, copy button |

## Copy rules

- One primary action per screen, named by what happens ("Reserve my seat"), not "Submit".
- Put the reassurance next to the button: cost, time needed, whether it can be cancelled.
- State what happens after sending ("You will get an email within a minute").
- Do not invent statistics or testimonials; leave a clearly marked placeholder instead.
